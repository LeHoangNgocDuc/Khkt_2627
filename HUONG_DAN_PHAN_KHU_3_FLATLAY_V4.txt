VISIONBOT V4.0 - CHE DO TRAI PHANG 2D / PHAN KHU 3
================================================

1. MO BANG DAI
- Truy cap website, bam menu "03 - Dau noi".
- Bang 2D SVG vector mo mac dinh, nen #111317 va luoi cham 20 px.
- Bam "TRINH CHIEU TV" de mo full screen.
- Bam "Xem mo hinh 3D" de chuyen sang ban 3D co san.

2. TRINH BAY CHO BAN GIAM KHAO
- Chon "TFT / SPI": xem GPIO42 -> SCL, GPIO14 -> SDA, GPIO1 -> RES,
  GPIO2 -> DC, GPIO21 -> CS, cung nhanh 3V3/GND/BLK.
- Chon "Nguon": xem pin Li-Po -> TP4056 -> cong tac -> Boost -> ESP32/Ampli.
- Chon "Am thanh I2S": xem GPIO39/40 dung chung xung Micro + Ampli,
  GPIO41 -> SD, GPIO47 -> DIN, SPK+/- -> hai cuc loa.
- Chon "Nut bam": GPIO38 -> Nut chan 1, GND -> nut chan 2.
- Bam vao tung so chan vang hoac duong day de phong to, hien cap dau noi.
- Bam nut bieu tuong mat de an/hien bang thong tin phia ben phai.

3. DIEU KHIEN
- Cuon chuot de Zoom; keo chuot de Pan; dap hai lan reset.
- Dien thoai: keo 1 ngon tay de Pan; chum 2 ngon tay de Zoom.
- Phim R: vua khit man hinh; +/-: phong/thu; [ / ]: doi day.
- Nut +, -, phong vua va mo/dong bang thong tin o goc phai man hinh.

4. DONG HO VAN NANG AO
- Do Pin Li-Po: hien 3.85V (gia lap).
- Do Boost: hien 5.02V khi ON, 0.00V khi OFF.
- Do 3V3: hien 3.30V khi ON, 0.00V khi OFF.
- Bam "Cong tac mo phong" de doi trang thai.
- Day LA SO LIEU MINH HOA, KHONG LAY TU THIET BI THAT.

5. AN TOAN
- Khong dua 5V vao chan 3V3 ESP32 / VDD INMP441.
- Khong cap pin Li-Po 1S vao chan ESP32 5V khong qua Boost.
- Khong noi SPK- hoac SPK+ xuong GND (ampli cau BTL).
- Khong dung the nho microSD khi GPIO38/39/40 dang lam viec khac.
- TP4056 thong thuong khong co quan ly duong nguon power-path;
  can xac minh model, mach bao ve, dong sac va cach ly USB.

6. TRANG THAI XAC THUC
- Toan bo 30 ket noi va header GPIO la CAU HINH DE XUAT.
- Da kiem tra duong day SVG dung tam pad, vuong goc va khong xuyen linh kien.
- Van phai doi chieu mach GOOUUU thuc te, ST7789, TP4056 va firmware.
- Theo tai lieu GOOUUU ESP32-S3-CAM, GPIO14 la chan dang co the dung cho
  MOSI/TFT; camera dung GPIO4-13 va GPIO15-18. GPIO38-40 trung microSD.
- Tranh cap nguon tu USB va Boost 5V dong thoi neu thieu bao ve nguon nguoc.
